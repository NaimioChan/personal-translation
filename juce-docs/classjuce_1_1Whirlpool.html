详细说明

Whirlpool 哈希类。

Whirlpool 算法由 Paulo S. L. M. Barreto 和 Vincent Rijmen 开发。

见 P.S.L.M. Barreto, V. Rijmen, "The Whirlpool hashing function" NESSIE submission, 2000 (tweaked version, 2001), https://www.cosic.esat.kuleuven.ac.be/nessie/workshop/submissions/whirlpool.zip

另见
SHA256, MD5
