librosa.util.sparsify_rows

librosa.util.sparsify_rows(x, *, quantile=0.01, dtype=None) [源码]

返回输入数组的行稀疏逼近

参数

参数类型说明
xnp.ndarray [ndim <= 2]

待稀疏化的输入数组。

quantilefloat in [0, 1.0)

在 x 的每一行中丢弃的幅度比例

dtypenp.dtype, optional

输出数组的数据类型。 若不提供，则使用 x.dtype。

返回值

返回值类型说明
x_sparsescipy.sparse.csr_array [shape=x.shape]

x 的行稀疏化逼近

若 x.ndim == 1，则把 x 视为行向量， 此时 x_sparse.shape == (1, len(x))。

异常

异常说明
ParameterError

若 x.ndim > 2

若 quantile 超出 [0, 1.0)

说明

本函数在层级 40 缓存。

示例

>>> # Construct a Hann window to sparsify
>>> x = scipy.signal.hann(32)
>>> x
array([ 0.   ,  0.01 ,  0.041,  0.09 ,  0.156,  0.236,  0.326,
        0.424,  0.525,  0.625,  0.72 ,  0.806,  0.879,  0.937,
        0.977,  0.997,  0.997,  0.977,  0.937,  0.879,  0.806,
        0.72 ,  0.625,  0.525,  0.424,  0.326,  0.236,  0.156,
        0.09 ,  0.041,  0.01 ,  0.   ])
>>> # Discard the bottom percentile
>>> x_sparse = librosa.util.sparsify_rows(x, quantile=0.01)
>>> x_sparse
<1x32 sparse array of type '<class 'numpy.float64'>'
    with 26 stored elements in Compressed Sparse Row format>
>>> x_sparse.toarray()
array([[ 0.   ,  0.   ,  0.   ,  0.09 ,  0.156,  0.236,  0.326,
         0.424,  0.525,  0.625,  0.72 ,  0.806,  0.879,  0.937,
         0.977,  0.997,  0.997,  0.977,  0.937,  0.879,  0.806,
         0.72 ,  0.625,  0.525,  0.424,  0.326,  0.236,  0.156,
         0.09 ,  0.   ,  0.   ,  0.   ]])
>>> # Discard up to the bottom 10th percentile
>>> x_sparse = librosa.util.sparsify_rows(x, quantile=0.1)
>>> x_sparse

    with 20 stored elements in Compressed Sparse Row format>

array([[ 0.   ,  0.   ,  0.   ,  0.   ,  0.   ,  0.   ,  0.326,
         0.424,  0.525,  0.625,  0.72 ,  0.806,  0.879,  0.937,
